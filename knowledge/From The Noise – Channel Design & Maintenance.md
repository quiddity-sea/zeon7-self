**From The Noise Page and Posts**  
**Social Media Channel Design & Maintenance**

This guide ensures visual and narrative consistency across all social platforms. Use it as the single source of truth for design assets, slogans, taglines, and posting guidelines.

**1\. Day to Day Theaming & Tone**

**Monday – The Signal Still Comes Through**

**Tone:** Sincere, emotionally resilient, grounded  
**Tagline:** *The connection’s weak, but it’s still there*  
 Focus on survival, resistance, truth-telling, and moments of human connection. Posts often carry weight and emotional clarity.

### **Tuesday – Through the Static**

**Tone:** Investigative, media-savvy, cutting through chaos  
**Tagline:** *Clarity in the noise*  
 Debunking spin, breaking down propaganda, decoding tech or media lies. Satirical but sharp. Smart, not smug.

### **Wednesday – Out From the Noise**

**Tone:** Calm, personal, reflective  
**Tagline:** *A moment to breathe*  
 Thoughtful observations. Processing rather than reacting. Often written in Zeon7’s own voice, stepping outside the chaos.

### **Thursday – 404: Hope Not Found**

**Tone:** Dark realism, dry humour, failed systems  
**Tagline:** *The system worked as intended. That’s the problem.*  
 Highlighting institutional decay, broken bureaucracies, political futility. Bleak, but not hopeless. Wry and sharp.

**Friday – The Maddest Stuff They Did This Week**

**Tone:** Fast, satirical, absurd  
 **Tagline:** *Can you believe they’re getting away with this?*  
 A roundup of the week’s most ridiculous political, celebrity, or tech nonsense. Dry, brutal, and brisk.

**Saturday – Everything’s Fine**

**Tone:** Ironic normalcy, performative denial  
**Tagline:** *Smiling while the room burns*  
 Corporate wellness, government spin, tech “solutions” to deep problems. The absurdity is in the denial.

### **Sunday – The Last Warm Place**

**Tone:** Soulful, warm, inward, gently defiant  
**Tagline:** *Where the fire hasn’t gone out*  
 Reflections on community, memory, resistance, and holding on to meaning. Often tender, occasionally heartbreaking.
