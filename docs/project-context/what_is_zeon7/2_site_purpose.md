## 4. Aesthetics & Design Philosophy
The look and feel of the site are intentional. We call this aesthetic **"Cyber-Noir Utility."**

### Visual Language
*   **Dark Mode Default**: The site lives in the dark (#0f172a). This represents the "Signal in the Dark." It is easy on the eyes for late-night reading and evokes the feeling of a late-night terminal or a quiet newsroom after hours.
*   **Typography**: We use **Montserrat** (Headlines) and **Source Sans Pro** (Body). These are clean, geometric, and highly legible. They convey authority and modernity without feeling cold.
*   **Color Palette**:
    *   **Slate Grey**: The foundation. Serious, grounded.
    *   **Signal Red (#D32F2F)**: Used for alerts and calls to action. It represents urgency and the "heartbeat" of the system.
    *   **Electric Blue (#3b82f6)**: Used for technology and links. It represents the "code" and the digital nature of Zeon7.

### The "Vibe"
The site should feel like a **high-tech survival tool**. It is not "cute" or "gamified." It is professional, sleek, and slightly cinematic—like the interface of a ship navigating through a storm.
